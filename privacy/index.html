Privacy

SeedMe · updated 24 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account — Google name, email, user ID.
  • Meals — photos, names and nutrition you log.
  • Health — with your permission, weight, body data, heart rate, steps and energy from Apple Health. Meals and weigh-ins are written back.
  • Location — with your permission, where you logged a meal.
  • Diagnostics — daily screens, taps and errors. Off in Settings.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database, storage.
  • Google Gemini, OpenAI — recognise food in meal photos.
  • Open Food Facts, USDA — nutrition lookups, no personal data.

Friends

See your name and marble. Today's meals only if you turn sharing on. Never your weight or history.

Deletion and your rights

Ask for a copy of your data, a fix or account deletion through Share Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Done within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Not for children under 16.
